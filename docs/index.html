How It Works

From counter photo to live Instagram post in four simple steps

1

Photo & Voice Note

The shopkeeper snaps a product on their glass counter and speaks naturally on Telegram in Tamil, Tanglish, Hindi, or English.

2

Multimodal Intelligence

Gemini 2.5 Flash transcribes the audio, extracts verified product facts, and writes a localized caption, hashtags, and image prompt.

3

Visual Restaging & QA

Wiro AI renders the product into a warm festival studio setting for $0.0280. Gemini QA reviews the image to prevent hallucinations.

4

Human Approval & Post

The owner taps Approve in Telegram. The post publishes to Instagram via Graph API and logs costs to Google Sheets.
